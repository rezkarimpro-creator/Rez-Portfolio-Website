## Rules for this site

- **Voice:** "we" is the studio speaking. Rez is the Design Lead and his friend is the Engineering Lead (name and photo only with his OK). No founder, co-founder, CEO or owner titles until Rez goes public (README).
- **Name:** never "DashBloom" on this site. The new name and domain are Rez's to choose; Claude can shortlist names with free domains when asked (spec §2).
- **Order:** the site launches with 5 main pages (home, services, work, about, contact) plus privacy and terms. The CMS pages come later, with 400 to 500 as the first target, gated by spec §10 (spec §8).
- **Quality:** every CMS page carries at least 3 blocks written for that page alone and one real visual. No invented stats, reviews, client logos or results. No city or "near me" pages (spec §8 to §10).
- **Development services:** those pages go live only after the dev partner agreement is signed (spec §13).
- **Benchmarks:** study only. Never name those agencies on the site.
- **Delivery format:** copy and page data in a format the friend's CMS can import. His acceptance checklist is spec §12.
