# Agency benchmarks: the top 50 and the best designs

[README](../README.md) · Researched 21 Oct 2026 · For the agency site in the [agency spec](01-agency-spec.md). Every name, price and number below comes from the agency's own site, its Clutch profile, a web search, or a visual review in BrowserOS, all made on 21 Oct 2026. The full data for all 132 agencies checked, with every sub-score, is in [03-agency-benchmarks.csv](03-agency-benchmarks.csv).

## 1. How to use this

- **Now:** your 5 main pages (home, services, work, about, contact). Section 2 gives the best example for each page, and the last column of section 4 says what to copy from each agency.
- **Looks:** section 5 lists the 20 best-looking agency sites, Afternow among them, picked by eye in BrowserOS. Start there for your page designs.
- **Later:** the CMS pages (400 to 500). Section 6 names the agencies that already run big page systems, and the one thing not to copy from them.
- **Top tier** agencies are there for the look: the most awards and famous clients, at any price. **Closest models** are there for structure, offers and prices: buyers and budgets like yours. Your eye decides the look.
- These names are for study only. None of them goes on your site.

## 3. How "best" was decided

**Checks.** An agency had to pass all four:

1. Active in 2026: the site is live in October 2026 and was updated in the last 12 months.
2. A real studio or agency, not a directory, marketplace or software tool.
3. Sells the category's service as a main offer.
4. Sells in English to US, UK or EU buyers.

**Score out of 100:**

| What | Points | How it was measured |
|---|---|---|
| Proof | 25 | Awards named on the site, or found in one search for big studios whose sites name none: up to 10 (Cannes Lions, D&AD, One Show, Clio, Webby, Red Dot, Awwwards, FWA, CSS Design Awards, Fast Company, European Design Awards, Webflow Awards). Known clients shown on the site: up to 8. Reviews: up to 7 (Clutch, or G2 or Trustpilot when those have more reviews) |
| Main pages | 25 | Headline says what and for whom (7) · proof on the home page (6) · prices or budget bands (6) · quote path with a button, a form and a booking link (6) |
| Search | 20 | 6 points for each of the category's searches where the agency's own page shows up, 2 for each mention in a "best agencies" list in those results |
| Fit | 20 | The category is its main offer (8) · buyers like yours: startups, B2B teams, small companies (6) · entry price close to your $500 to $3,000 packages (6) |
| Ready for later | 10 | Size of the English sitemap (5) · service, industry or other page types beyond a blog (5) |

**Picking.** In each category, the top tier slots go to the highest awards plus clients score, with ties going to more awards and then the higher total. The closest model slots go to the highest total among the rest, with ties going to the better fit. Slots: brand 5 + 5, websites 4 + 6, decks 3 + 5, social 2 + 4, development 2 + 4, monthly plans 2 + 4, apps 2 + 2. That makes 20 top tier and 30 closest models.

**Sources.** 31 category searches built from the target queries in [02-seo-pages-phase1.csv](02-seo-pages-phase1.csv) · a script that read each agency's home, about, contact, pricing and work pages plus its sitemap · 64 Clutch profiles · 14 award and review searches · 5 extra page reads where client logos had no names. No paid tools and no logins were used.

**Limits.**

- My search tool shows US results that are close to Google's but not the same. Please spot-check a few searches in your own browser.
- 11 sites could not be read from here (blocked, down or parked). They are marked "Not checked" and were not scored. SlideGenius and The Branx are the two worth a manual look.
- Awards count only when the site names them or one search finds them. A studio that wins a lot but never lists it scores low here. Pentagram is the clearest case.
- Main pages were judged on text and structure, not visuals. Section 5 adds the visual review.
- Clutch numbers change, and many famous studios and small deck studios have no Clutch reviews at all.
- Offer and fit points are my reading of each site. The CSV shows every number, so any of them can be changed and the list re-ranked.

## 5. Best designs to see

Added 21 Oct 2026, after you pointed out Afternow (formerly BB Agency). This section ranks on design alone. I opened every home page in BrowserOS and judged what a visitor sees in the first screen and on the way down.

**Why the first pass missed them**

- **Where the names came from.** The 50 came from buyer searches ("brand identity design agency" and the like) and "best agency" lists. Studios known for their design rarely rank for those generic searches or show up in paid lists, so most never entered the pool.

- **No points for looks.** The score judged main pages on text and structure, and I didn't open the sites in a browser. A beautiful site with a short headline and no prices scored low.
- **Awwwards wasn't read.** My one Awwwards search found nothing, so its 2025 winners (Immersive Garden, Agency of the Year; Malvah, Studio of the Year) were never checked.

## 6. Patterns across the 50

- **Prices in public.** 30 of the 50 show prices or budget bands. Among the closest models it is 23 of 30, and among the top tier only 7 of 20. Your "from" prices in [agency spec §11](01-agency-spec.md#11-offers-and-prices) put you with the agencies that win clients your size.
