## 1. How I pick links for a job

1. Match the job type first, then the industry
2. 1 to 3 links per application. Never more
3. Lead with client work. A concept is only called a concept
4. No clear match means the generic profile link
5. The codes used are logged in the platform file under `jobs/` and in `tracker.md`, for example `D1, B1`
6. Links are pasted in a clean form. Behance resolves a project by its number, so `behance.net/gallery/255544227/Earworm-Investor-Pitch-Deck` opens D1 without the brackets in the original link
