## 1. The goal

- Become a multi-millionaire within a few years, starting now. That takes income that compounds (recurring clients and a team), not just more billed hours. Billing hours alone tops out near $100k a year (02-plan §1).
- On the way: a second income before Taste's slide work may end (16–30 Nov), then the targets in §6.

| Signal | Source |

| Complex work done with AI: earnings up 45%. Freelancers who use AI earn 34% more per hour | Same |
| Fiverr buyers down 22% in a year, but spend per buyer up 16% and $1k+ projects up 13% | Fiverr Q2 2026 results |
| Fiverr searches: brand identity up 26%, logo up 76%, Canva work up 403% (interest, not sales) | Fiverr Business Trends Index, June 2026 |

| Cross-border hiring of AI trainers up 283% in 2025 | Deel, cited in the Fiverr/Upwork article |

What it means:
1. People still want design. What has collapsed is the price of simple one-off work: cheap logos, template slides, single posts.
2. The money moved to bigger jobs where one person owns the whole result: a brand system, a website and its launch, done fast with AI.
3. Sell packages from $1k up.
4. AI training contracts are the fastest cash right now (Mercor, Micro1).

## 8. Positioning

- **Headline:** "Brand & website designer for startups and B2B teams · Brand systems, websites, pitch decks · Designed the deck used in Earworm's £200K pre-seed".
- **Why:** demand for brand and web work is holding, and most of your work is there. Decks stay, third, for high-stakes decks that AI handles badly. Use AI openly in your own work, never on Taste work; people who do are the ones earning more.
- **Portfolio vs agency:** the portfolio sells you (jobs, freelance, agencies); the agency site sells a team (search, bigger packages). You're "Design Lead" on the agency site until the studio out-earns a job for 3 months running.
- **Keeping them apart:** quiet links only (the agency's team section links to your portfolio, and the portfolio mentions the agency in About); the same project gets two different write-ups; Fiverr isn't named on either site; the agency never sells templates in its own name.

## 9. Risks

| Risk | What the plan does |
|---|---|
| AI keeps pushing prices down for routine work | Sell outcomes and packages; use AI for speed |
| Sales stop when delivery gets busy | The daily send block stays; hire a project manager before the team reaches 10 |
| Monthly plans churn | Lead with agencies, which stay longer; review every client each quarter |
| Hiring quality (your current designer is weak) | Paid trials, written checklists, you review every delivery |
| Selling development nobody can deliver | Build pages go live only after the agreement with your friend |
| Google ignores mass pages | 101 pages first, real proof on each, an indexing gate, pruning after 90 days |
| Fiverr keeps shrinking | $1k+ packages only; never the main channel |
| Taste ends early | Pipeline mode (02-plan §7); the warm network and agencies are already running |
| Taste confidentiality | Nothing from Taste on either site, ever |
