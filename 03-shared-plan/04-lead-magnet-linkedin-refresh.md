[README](../README.md) · Locked 19 Oct 2026 · Warm outreach. Hormozi's early move: free work for a few people, in return for proof.

## 1. Why it works

- Your LinkedIn has 4 recommendations, all from 2020 classmates and none from clients (01-profile §6). Client recommendations help with jobs, freelance work and agencies alike.
- Hormozi's type: a sample, done by you. It fixes one narrow problem (a dated profile) and reveals the next one: next to the new profile, the company's brand or website looks dated. That's the 21-Day Brand & Website Launch.
- About an hour per client once you have a Figma master.
- It also completes 02-plan step 9 ("Ask 3 past clients for a LinkedIn recommendation").

## 2. Rules

- **Deliver first, then ask, one ask per stage.** Every ask is optional and easy to decline; the offer says "no strings".
- **They upload the files themselves.** Never ask for a login.
- **Message rules** from the leads folder: short and warm, no em dash, colon, semicolon or hyphen (file names are exempt), no prices, signed "Rezaul".
- **You send everything.** Claude doesn't read their profiles; you check them by hand.
- **Direct clients only.** Clients who came through Fiverr stay on Fiverr (03-playbook §2).

## 6. Upload guide

Send it as a PDF, or paste it in. It follows the message rules. Rename the files for each client.

```text
Your new LinkedIn look, about 5 minutes on a computer

Profile photo
1. Open your profile and click your photo
2. Choose to change it, upload photo.png, then save

Banner
1. Click the camera or pencil icon on your banner
2. Upload banner.png, check the crop, then apply

Featured
1. Find Featured under your intro. If it isn't there, click Add profile section, then Recommended, then Add featured
2. Click the plus and choose the option written in notes.txt, Add media or Add a link
3. Upload featured-1.png or paste the link, add the title from notes.txt, then save
4. Do the same for tile 2, then drag the tiles into order

Last check
Open your profile on your phone and make sure the banner and photo look right.
LinkedIn moves its buttons now and then, so the names may differ a little.
```

## 7. Before you send a preview

- [ ] Show it as a full LinkedIn profile mockup: photo, banner and Featured together, on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Screenshot their current profile on desktop and mobile first. That's the "before".
- [ ] Check the banner on mobile. LinkedIn crops it there, and the photo covers part of it.
- [ ] Sizes: banner 1584 × 396 px; photo at least 400 × 400 px, square (LinkedIn shows it as a circle).
- [ ] Featured: per tile, **Media** (your tile shows) or **Link** (LinkedIn may show the website's own preview instead). Write the choice, the title and the link into notes.txt.
- [ ] Check that you're connected on LinkedIn. Recommendations need a connection.
