# Templates: products and lead magnets

[README](../README.md) · Locked 19 Oct 2026 · Builds on two plan-repo briefs from 18 Oct: `briefs/2026-10-18_etsy-template-shops.md` (shops, payouts, the US partner) and `briefs/2026-10-18_etsy-digital-products-research.md` (what sells).

## 1. Two jobs

1. **Products** that sell on marketplaces while you work on other things.
2. **Lead magnets:** #6 (10-Slide Story Deck) and #7 (LinkedIn Profile Kit) are free templates that bring founders into your email list ([02-funnel §4](02-funnel.md#4-the-7-lead-magnets)).

AI tools make templates too, so templates stay a side line your designer runs, not a core.

## 2. Where templates live

| Place | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy (the US partner is the seller), Creative Market, Canva Creators | Sales. Their search brings the buyers | Partner and applications still open |
| Gumroad (rezkarim) | The free templates #6 and #7, and the email list | Set up alongside the portfolio |
| Your portfolio's /free page | Explains the free templates, links to Gumroad | Portfolio v1 |
| The agency site's /templates/ pages | One page per template: previews, formats, links to its listings, "Want us to customize it?" | Phase 2 |

The agency never sells templates in its own name; its pages only link to the listings.

## 3. The ladder

Free template (Gumroad) → paid template $6–30 → "We customize it" $99–199 → 72-Hour Deck Rescue or 21-Day Brand & Website Launch.

## 4. Upsell rules per shop

- **Etsy** bans steering Etsy buyers to buy outside Etsy (its fee avoidance policy). So the upsell there is an Etsy custom listing, and files sold on Etsy carry no outside links.
- **Creative Market and Canva:** read each one's rules before putting any link inside a file.
- **Gumroad and your own sites:** links to the call and the offers are fine.

## 5. Promotion (locked 19 Oct)

The 18 Oct rule ("no marketing until a shop sells") stays for anything that costs time or money, with two near-free exceptions: Pinterest, and the free templates on LinkedIn.

| Channel | What | When | Your time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace search | Titles, tags, first image, listing count; Claude writes the listings | From the first listing | None |
| Pinterest | 3–5 pins per listing through the post pipeline's native scheduler | From 10 live listings | Batch approval |
| LinkedIn | The free templates #6 and #7, offered in posts; the link goes by DM after a real comment | Once they're live | Inside the 3 posts a week |
| Instagram, Facebook | The Pinterest images as cross-posts, only if the pipeline does it automatically | With Pinterest | None |
| Paid ads | None | The day-60 review (~17 Dec) decides | — |

Never on your LinkedIn: resume, Instagram or media-kit templates. Your buyers there are founders and B2B teams.
