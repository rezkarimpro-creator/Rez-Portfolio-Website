## The plan in one screen

1. **Sell every service, three ways, with one team:** projects (brand, websites, decks, social, LinkedIn), then monthly design plans, then white-label work for agencies.
2. **Fast cash first, then grow.** Contracts (Taste, Mercor) and warm-network projects pay now. Agency retainers and monthly plans build the recurring base from December.
3. **AI is your team's speed.** Same price, faster delivery, better margin. Never on Taste work.
4. **Clients come from, in this order:** warm network, agencies, startup outreach, directories (Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush), LinkedIn content, SEO. Fiverr only with $1k+ packages.
5. **Seven lead magnets**, built on Hormozi's three types: free work done by you for one-to-one outreach, and a scorecard plus two templates for content.
6. **A realistic road:** about $10k a month by Mar 2027, $25–35k by Dec 2027, $80–100k ($1M+ a year) by 2029.
7. **Websites:** your portfolio first, built in its own folder and tested on one of your domains. Then the agency: new name and look, you design 5 page templates, your friend builds the rest.
