## Notes

- **The floor for a .com is about $18 to $19 over two years.** Anything lower means a promo code (Spaceship COM67 lead) or another extension. The best-looking cheap alternatives are .fyi (10.40), .cc (11.95) and .work (12.87), saving $7 to $9 against .com over two years.
- **Promo jumps.** Any price marked "sale", "promo" or "code" lasts one year. Flat prices (Cloudflare .com, .fyi, .link, .page, .net, .me; Porkbun .fyi, .link, .com) don't jump. Cloudflare .dev rises from 8.20 to 12.20.
- **Cloudflare prices** come from cfdomainpricing.com, a third-party mirror of Cloudflare's list, updated 21 Oct 2026. Cloudflare's own page shows prices only inside the dashboard. The .com figure (10.46) fits registry price plus the ICANN fee. Rez sees the exact price at checkout.
- **Not checked:** Spaceship prices for TLDs other than .com, .org, .net; GoDaddy; transfer fees (they matter only if Rez moves the domain later).
- **.dev, .page and .foo** force HTTPS for every visitor. Cloudflare handles that, so it is not a problem here.
- **rezaulkarim.com** expires 20 Nov 2026. If it drops, it would show as available at any registrar. Worth one look in late Nov or Dec, but not worth waiting for.
