## Rules for this site

- **Voice:** "I", Rez as a designer. Never "we" meaning a studio, never founder or owner. DashBloom appears only as "Studio: DashBloom" on cases done there, and as a past employer in About, Jan 2024 to Sep 2026 (spec §6, row 78).
- **Cases:** codes and order as in spec §4. Its labels are internal: the site shows no Client, Concept or To confirm tags and no years on any case (row 81). Earworm (D1) and the Real Estate website (W1) always sit above SolVita. NDA work stays off, with no NDA card (row 60). Taste Labs never appears, not even its title (row 61).
- **Prices** only on /work-with-me. /work-with-me, /agencies and the whole test site stay noindex (spec §9).
- **Code rules:** for work inside `site/`, the rules in `site/AGENTS.md` apply; Claude reads them through `site/CLAUDE.md`. Keep the two in step: business rules stay here, code and content rules live there (decision 3).
- **Stack:** `site/` runs Next.js 16 with Cache Components off (Rez picked Next.js on 21 Oct; decision record 0003 in `site/docs/`), git from day one. It needs a server for its form, passwords and visit counter, so it runs on Cloudflare Workers through OpenNext, with visits and messages in Cloudflare storage (D1, Rez's call, row 91) and `.data` on localhost. Tested on one of Rez's existing domains until rezkarim.com is bought. Rez does every account, DNS and deploy step himself (shared hard rule 5).
- **CVs:** the three CVs never appear on the site (Rez, 21 Oct). Recruiters get one "Ask for my CV" path to the Recruiter form.
- **Look:** interactive and Awwwards style, but very fast and smooth (build notes §1). Borrow patterns from the board, never copy a site 1:1.
- **Approval:** each step of the build process (spec §8) needs Rez's yes before it starts.
- **Preview:** `.claude/launch.json` at the project root holds "portfolio-site" (port 3000); preview_start with that name starts the dev server. Check every page in BrowserOS, localhost included, in your own background tab (row 71). Screenshots need the focus and screencast step in the root CLAUDE.md. Never the built-in preview pane or Claude in Chrome.
- **Board picks:** they live in the localStorage of the browser Rez used (key `rez-inspiration-picks`). If they don't show after the move, ask him to press "Copy picks" in his browser and paste them into the chat.
