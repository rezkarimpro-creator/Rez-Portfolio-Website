# Client quote request

[README](../README.md) · Draft · 29 Oct 2026 · Chat R · Rez sends this himself, by email or LinkedIn DM. Nothing is sent from here.

## Why

The site has one quote (Earworm). Rez is collecting 3 to 5 named client quotes (README §2). Each needs the client's own words, name, role, company and a clear OK to publish. The coordinator passes them to chat C's proof block.

## The message (email)

Subject line: A small favour, if you have a minute

Hi [first name],

I'm adding a few client words to my new site, rezkarim.com, and yours would mean a lot. Working on [the project] with you was a real pleasure.

Could you send me two or three sentences about what it was like to work together and what changed for you after? Please add your name, your role and your company, and tell me it's fine for me to publish your words on my site and profiles.

No rush, and please be honest. Thank you.

Rez

## Short version (LinkedIn DM or WhatsApp)

Hi [first name], I'm adding a few client words to my new site, rezkarim.com. Could you send two or three sentences about working together, with your name, role and company, and your OK to publish them? Thank you.

## Before sending

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Fill in the first name and the project, nothing else | The message is the same for everyone |
| Past clients only. Earworm and others Rez names | Rez picks who. Delineate (YC W25) only if Rez wants to ask them |
| Never offer anything in return, never write their words for them | Quotes must be the client's own |
| Keep their reply. A forwarded email or screenshot is the proof of OK | Hard rule 4: provable only |
| Pass the quote to the coordinator with name, role, company and the OK | Chat C's quotes block stays empty until then |

Copy rules: no em dash, colon, semicolon or hyphen in the message body; no Fiverr; no claim about results.
